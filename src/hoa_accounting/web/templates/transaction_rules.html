What rules do

When you import a bank statement, each transaction's description is compared against your saved rules. A match automatically records and posts the transaction so you don't have to categorize it by hand.

How matching works

Each criteria field you fill in must match — they are combined with AND logic. You can match on any combination of Description, Transaction Type, Memo, and Amount. Leave all blank and the rule matches every transaction (useful as a catch-all). All text matches are case-insensitive and check if the value appears anywhere in the field.

Match priority: Rules win over existing matches, which win over batch grouping. A transaction can only be claimed by one rule.

Active / inactive

Toggle a rule Off to suspend it without deleting it — handy for seasonal vendors or accounts that change periodically.

The four transaction types
↑ Recurring Bill / Auto-Pay
Money leaving the account for a known vendor: utilities, insurance, landscaping, management fees.
↑ Bank Fee or Charge
Service fees, wire charges, and NSF fees charged by the bank itself.
↓ Dues or Assessment Payment
Deposits from homeowners for monthly dues or special assessments. Optionally assigns a specific Lot so the deposit is recorded as an AR payment and applied to any open assessments for that homeowner.
↓ Fee or Other Income
Late fees, resale certificate fees, refunds, and interest earned.
Lot assignment (dues rules only)

When you pick a Lot / Homeowner, the import engine creates a payment receipt and automatically applies it to that lot's oldest open assessments, marking them Partial or Paid. If no open assessments exist the receipt is still recorded and can be applied later. Leave the lot blank if the deposit covers multiple owners.
