Your Account Category List
Every HOA needs a list of categories for money coming in and going out — things like Dues & Assessments, Landscaping, Insurance, and Reserve Fund. Accountants call this a "Chart of Accounts." You can call it your category list.

We've already built one for you. It's a standard set used by HOAs just like yours. You don't need to do anything — just click the green button below and you'll have a complete, working set of categories. You can add, rename, or remove any of them later — this is just a starting point.

Or, if you prefer, use our Setup Wizard — it asks you a few plain-English questions like "How many bank accounts do you have?" and builds your list automatically. No accounting knowledge needed.
