{# ── Golden rules ── #}
Golden Rules
  • Dues income is recognized when billed, not when cash arrives. Collecting a late fee reduces AR — it does not post new income.
  • Edit through Fix & Edit — corrections are allowed, but every change is recorded in the Audit Log. Don't edit the database directly.
  • Reserve funds need a board resolution before any transfer back to operating.
  • Close periods promptly — open periods from prior months are a red flag. Closing locks the books and prevents accidental edits.
  • Reconcile every month — a reconciled balance is the only way to know your book balance matches the bank.
  • Backup before fiscal-year rollover — download a full database backup before opening the new year. Use Admin → Database.
